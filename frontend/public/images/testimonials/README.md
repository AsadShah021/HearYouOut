# Testimonial photos

Optional. Faces make the quotes on the homepage read as real people rather than
invented marketing copy.

Filenames are set per-testimonial in the `photo` field in
`src/lib/data/marketing.ts`. Any testimonial without a photo falls back to the
initials mark.

## Specs

- **Square**, at least **200×200** (they render at 40–48px).

## Before launch

Only use a photo where the person actually gave that quote and agreed to have
their face on your site. Attaching a stock face to a review is a fabricated
endorsement — the same problem as stock listener photos, with added legal risk.
